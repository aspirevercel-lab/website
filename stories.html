Stories

Real stories, shared with care.

Stories help people understand what person-directed support can look like in everyday life. Each story is shared respectfully, with the person's voice and dignity at the centre.

What stories can show

Every story begins with the person.

  • Choices people are making for their own lives.
  • Confidence growing at home and in the community.
  • New skills, friendships, routines, or opportunities.
  • Support that listens first and works alongside whānau.
