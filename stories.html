Stories

Real stories, shared with care.

Stories help people understand what person-directed support can look like in everyday life. Every story should be shared with consent, dignity, and privacy in mind.

Story guidelines

Trust comes from respectful storytelling.

  • Use only stories that have clear consent from the person and/or their authorised representative.
  • Avoid sensitive health, family, financial, or behavioural details.
  • Focus on goals, choices, strengths, community connection, and what matters to the person.
  • Use first names, changed names, or no names depending on consent.
