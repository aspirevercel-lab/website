Feedback & complaints

Your voice helps us improve.

Feedback, concerns, compliments, and complaints are important. We want people and whānau to feel heard, respected, and safe to speak up.

How to raise something

Start with the person or channel you feel comfortable with.

  • Talk with an Aspire team member, support manager, or senior leader.
  • Email reception@aspire.org.nz or call 07 839 0183.
  • Tell us what happened, what matters to you, and the best way to contact you.
  • You can ask a trusted person, advocate, or whānau member to support you.
Contact Aspire
What happens next

We take concerns seriously.

We listen

We will listen to what happened, what matters to you, and what outcome you are seeking.

We respond

The right person will follow up, gather information where needed, and explain the process.

We improve

Feedback helps us strengthen quality, safety, rights, and wellbeing across Aspire.

Rights and advocacy

People have the right to speak up.

Aspire supports self-advocacy and the Code of Rights. We encourage people to ask questions, seek support, and use advocacy when they want help to raise an issue or understand their rights.
