Funding & eligibility

Understanding support pathways can make the next step easier.

Aspire services may be accessed through different assessment, funding, and referral pathways depending on a person's needs and circumstances.

Starting point

Eligibility is usually assessed before services begin.

If you or someone you know needs support, eligibility is usually assessed through Disability Support Link or another regional needs assessment service. Some pathways may involve ACC, Enabling Good Lives, Health New Zealand, MSD, Whaikaha, or related disability support funding.
