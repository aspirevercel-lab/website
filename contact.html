Donate to Aspire - Community Support

Aspire - Community Support is a not-for-profit charitable trust. Donations help make meaningful projects possible for the people we support.

Like many non-profits, we have a wish list of projects we would love to complete, but need additional funds to get underway. If you would like to donate to support one of these projects, please contact our General Manager - Finance & Infrastructure for more information. All donations to charities like ours are tax deductible.

Some of our discretionary projects are:

  • Volunteer reimbursement for expenses and mileage.
  • Wheelchair weigh scale.
  • Beautification of our properties.
  • Purchase of a trampoline for one of the houses where several young people live.
We also welcome enquiries from people who may like to leave a legacy gift to Aspire in their will.
