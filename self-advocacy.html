Self advocacy

Your voice matters.

Self-advocacy means speaking up for yourself, knowing your rights and responsibilities, and making choices about your life.

Speaking up for yourself

Self-advocacy means you can ask for what you need, share your thoughts and feelings, and tell people what you want for your life. It does not mean you cannot ask for help. It means you are in control of your decisions, and you are responsible for them.

  • Large group meetings several times a year about the Code of Rights and important topics.
  • A self-advocacy subcommittee that meets every two months.
  • Monthly self-advocacy meetings in each Aspire house.
