Foundation

Community participation that helps people explore, learn, and succeed.

Foundation is for people in New Zealand with a disability or long-term health condition who need extra support to live their best life after finishing school.

What we help with

Find and do what you want to do.

Life Skills

Travel, money, cooking, personal care, emotional health, communication, friendships, wellbeing, and decision-making.

Community

Volunteering, clubs, local groups, and active participation as valued community members.

Wellness

Yoga, sport, walking groups, gymnastics, swimming, bowling, fun runs, and other wellbeing activities.

Employment

Training and support to build skills, confidence, and readiness for employment pathways.

Our commitment

Values that guide the work.

Equity of access

Break down barriers and open pathways so everyone can participate fully.

Encouragement

Inspire confidence, celebrate progress, and turn challenges into opportunities.

Empathy

Listen deeply, honour experiences, and respond with understanding and respect.

Engagement

Work together in true partnership with participants, families, and communities.

Empowerment

Build confidence, share tools, and support people to shape their futures.

Excellence

Strive for high standards, innovation, and ongoing improvement.

How to get started

Three simple steps.

Fill in the form

Share your name, details, and how and when you would like us to contact you.

We contact you

We will email or phone you and ask if you would like to meet with us.

We support you

We organise support in the community to help you do what you want.
