What we offer

Support that adapts to each person's life.

Our services are person-directed, relationship-based, and designed to support safety, independence, skill-building, and community connection.

Residential Support

Living life, your way

Our Community Residential service provides a home and 24/7 support for children and adults with disabilities across the Waikato region. These homes are more than places to live. They are spaces where people feel safe, supported, and truly at home.

  • Person-directed support tailored to goals, needs, and preferences.
  • Strong, respectful relationships with Aspire team members.
  • Daily support that encourages independence, skills, and community involvement.
Flexible Disability Support

Your life, your way

Everything starts with the person. Aspire provides flexible, tailored support that fits your life, whether you are starting your journey or shaping existing supports.

  • Highly trained team members supported by experienced leaders.
  • Office-based administration support so people can focus on living meaningfully.
  • A conversation-led approach where goals and preferences shape the support plan.
Supported Living

Support in your own home

Supported Living is provided to people in their own homes. It is a lower level of support than Community Residential, usually involving fewer hours and less intensive support.

  • Aspire staff partner with each person and provide support where it is needed.
  • This service is funded by Whaikaha, Ministry of Disabled People.
  • Download the SIL flier once the file has been added.
How to access Aspire

We can guide you through the process.

We know that choosing the right support service can feel overwhelming. That is why we are here to guide you and your whānau through the process, answering questions, exploring your options, and helping you decide whether Aspire is the right fit.

If you or someone you know needs support, eligibility is usually assessed through Disability Support Link or another regional needs assessment service.
