Services at a glance

Aspire services are funded through agencies including Health New Zealand, MSD, ACC, Enabling Good Lives, and Whaikaha-funded support pathways.

Residential Support

24/7 support in community homes where people feel safe, connected, and encouraged to live well.

Supported Independent Living

Lower-level support in a person's own home, partnering wherever support is needed.

Community Participation

Foundation helps people explore activities, relationships, learning, wellness, and work goals.

Our philosophy

We are committed to believing in the best of people and focusing on developing their skills and abilities. We believe that all people have a valuable role to play in their community and that when people are placed at the heart of everything we do, we can only get it right.

Accessing Aspire

The next step can start with a simple conversation.

Choosing the right support service can feel overwhelming. We can guide you and your whānau through the process, answer questions, explore options, and help you decide whether Aspire is the right fit.

  • Eligibility is usually assessed through Disability Support Link or another regional needs assessment service.
  • Services may be funded through Health New Zealand, MSD, ACC, Enabling Good Lives, Whaikaha, or related disability support pathways.
  • No matter where you are in your journey, we are here to help make the next steps easier.
