Make a referral

Help someone take the next step toward support.

Whether you are enquiring for yourself, a whānau member, or someone you support professionally, Aspire can help you understand the next step.

Who can enquire

Referrals can start from many places.

  • Disabled people seeking support for themselves.
  • Families, whānau, guardians, or trusted supporters.
  • Needs assessment services, ACC, EGL connectors, support coordinators, and health or social service professionals.
  • Schools, transition teams, or providers supporting young people moving into adult services.
What helps us respond

Information to include

Person and contact details

Name, preferred contact method, location, and who is making the enquiry.

Support being explored

Residential, SIL, ACC, EGL, Thrive, VHN, YPD, Foundation, or another pathway.

Funding or assessment

Any known NASC, ACC, EGL, Health New Zealand, MSD, or Whaikaha information.

What happens next

We listen first.

After you contact Aspire, we will listen to what is needed, discuss possible service fit, and guide you toward the right pathway. If another agency needs to assess eligibility first, we can help explain what that means.

For urgent health or safety concerns, contact the appropriate emergency or crisis service. Aspire enquiries are responded to through normal organisational channels.
