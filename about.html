About Aspire

Supporting people to live independently in their communities since 1989.

Aspire grew from the move away from large institutions and continues to evolve alongside more flexible models of disability support in Aotearoa New Zealand.

Our story

Agile, responsive, and committed to meaningful outcomes.

Established in 1989 in response to the closure of large institutions, Aspire began with a vision to support people to live independently within their communities. In 2015, we became Aspire - Community Support, reflecting our growth into a dynamic, forward-thinking service.

Today, we offer a range of living options across multiple properties, backed by a diverse and skilled team. We work closely with the people we support, our team, and others in the disability sector to achieve meaningful outcomes.

Governance

Our Board

As a not-for-profit organisation, Aspire Community Support is governed by a dedicated Board of Trustees who provide strategic oversight and guide our long-term direction. Their focus is on the big picture, ensuring our values, vision, and goals remain at the heart of everything we do.
